write_it_up v1.0.0

Ship What You Write

Every project on this page started as a first prompt. Members write, iterate with Claude, and keep going until it's something real.

Built a site, a tool, or a bot? Share it with the club and it can be featured here, with its own page, screenshots, and credit to the people who built it.

We also want to send member projects to Anthropic. The best ones go in front of the team at Anthropic, so what you build here can reach the people who made the tools.

You don't need to be a developer to ship. Start small, show people what you made, and learn from the other student builders around you.
