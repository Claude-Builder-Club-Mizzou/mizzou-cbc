Meet Claude

Claude is an AI assistant built by Anthropic. Use it to write, research, analyze, brainstorm, and learn on any project. It's the tool behind everything our club does.

Claude Code takes that further. It lives in your terminal, reads your project, and helps you plan, write, debug, and ship real software — even if you've never coded before.

Through the Claude API, you can also build Claude into your own apps. Our workshops walk you through all three, from your first prompt to your first deployed project.
